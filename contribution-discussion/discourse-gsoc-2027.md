Title: AiiDA will not participate in GSoC 2027

Hi everyone,

Unfortunately, the AiiDA team won't be able to apply as a mentoring organization for Google Summer of Code 2027.

Our active maintainer team has become much smaller than in previous years, and nearly all remaining capacity is reserved for the current AiiDA v3 rewrite. Good mentorship needs sustained, week-to-week guidance, and we currently cannot provide that.

Bug reports and design discussions stay welcome on [GitHub issues](https://github.com/aiidateam/aiida-core/issues) and here on Discourse. We hope to return as a mentoring organization in a future edition.
